FOR KITE SCHOOLS AND CLUBS

The kite trip your crew
will still be talking about in ten years

Bilene, Mozambique. A 15 km enclosed lagoon, warm water and wind from September to March. And if you get the group together, your trip is on us.

THE SPOT

Flat water, and room to breathe

— Kilometres of standing-depth water, about a metre deep. Beginners can stand up and walk back to their board, everyone else gets flat water all the way to the horizon.

— No tides, no reef, no shore break. Walk in and go.

— Wind nine days out of ten, steady thermals from September to March.

— Never more than twelve kiters on the water. That’s our record, not our average.

Leave the wetsuit at home. Boardies will do.

WHERE YOU STAY

From the sofa to the water:
thirty seconds

Our own houses, right on the lagoon. We timed it: thirty seconds in flip-flops, twenty-two if you run.

THE GROUP

Everyone round
the same table at night

You don’t have to plan the evenings: the Bilene Kite Center team has something different lined up every night. Local restaurants, braais on the beach…

THE CENTRE

Seven years of knowing this spot

— We’ve been in Bilene for seven years, and we know what each rider needs at every level.

— Lessons are run by IKO-certified instructors.

— Everything’s on site: kit storage, showers, and a beach crew who rig your kit and put it away for you.

Yes, you read that right: no rigging, no de-rigging. We do it – with the same care you’d give your own kit.

BRING YOUR CREW

We cover your trip.

Bring three people and your stay costs you nothing: accommodation, transfers and kit storage included. Bring ten or more and we’ll chip in on your flight too.
